Free extra input

On adds a “Something else” pill to every question. It opens a short text field for that question’s own answer, which goes into the draft. On a phone, add ?free=on to the URL.

When you press Write my text

Gets stuck never sends a word: it slows down, then fails. Try again works.

Jump to a state

Jumped-to states hold still for review. Press Write it with AI in the phone to see it live.

Flow

  • Write it with AI turns into a question card under the field. The header folds into a compact “About you” bar so the field and the card fit on one screen.
  • One question at a time. “Question 1/3” with up to 2 picks; the rest dim once 2 are picked. With Free extra input on, a Something else pill opens a short text field for that question.
  • Next question moves on, Skip clears that question and moves on. On the last question the button becomes Write my text and the card gives way to the draft.
  • Closing the card (× or Esc) writes nothing and unfolds the header; picks are kept.
  • Based on your answers. After a draft, a summary of the picks sits above the field. Edit reopens the card at question 1 with the picks selected (disabled while writing).
  • Write again writes another wording from the same picks. Undo returns the field to what it held before and unfolds the header.

States

Idle, generating, slow, stuck and done work as in the Input content AI generation component.
