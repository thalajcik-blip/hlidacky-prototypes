Flow

  • Write it with AI opens the sheet instead of writing straight away. The step underneath stays as it was.
  • Three questions, all on one sheet. Up to 2 picks each; once 2 are picked, the rest dim. The counter shows “N of 2”. Something else opens a short text field.
  • Write my text closes the sheet and the draft streams into the field, written from the picks. Skip questions writes from the previous steps only.
  • Closing without writing. Drag the handle down, tap the dimmed area or press Esc — nothing is written, picks are kept for next time.
  • Based on your answers. After a draft, a summary of the picks sits above the field. Edit reopens the sheet with them selected (disabled while writing).
  • Write again writes another wording from the same picks. Undo returns the field to what it held before.

States

Idle, generating, slow, stuck and done work as in the Input content AI generation component.
