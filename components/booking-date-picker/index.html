Booking date picker

Four ways to offer the days a parent usually books this sitter. Variant 1 puts them as pills under the date, with any other day in a plain dropdown list (Figma). Variant 2 opens a bottom sheet with the suggestions on top and any other day in a weekly strip or a full month (Figma). Variant 3 is the same sheet with the suggestions as pills and reworked day states. Variant 4 moves the pills out onto the form, as in Variant 1, and keeps the sheet just for picking a day (Figma).
