A textarea that can draft its own content with AI. The text streams into the field the user will edit, and the field shows every state on the way: writing, taking longer, stuck, done and stopped. Taken from the About me step of the sitter profile wizard.

Behaviour

  • Draft in place. Words stream into the same textarea the user edits, not into a preview. The field grows with the text (116–480 px) and the step scrolls so the status and Stop stay above the footer.
  • Nothing is lost. Undo restores what the user had written before the first draft; Try again and Generate again replace AI text only. A stuck draft keeps whatever arrived.
  • Busy means busy. While writing, the field is read-only with aria-busy="true", and Next and Back are disabled so the step cannot be left with a half-written text.
  • Announcements. The status line is a polite live region; the stuck alert is role="alert". Focus goes to Stop when writing starts, to Try again when it gets stuck and into the field when it is done.
  • Timings. First word after ~0.7 s, then 30–80 ms per word. Slow after 3.5 s without a word, stuck after 8 s. Real values should come from the model’s latency.
  • Reduced motion. The ring and the dots stand still; the text still streams.
