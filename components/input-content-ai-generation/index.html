Behaviour

  • Draft in place. Words stream into the same textarea the user edits, not into a preview. The field keeps its 148 px height and scrolls to the newest line.
  • No partial failures. The text either arrives or the request fails before the first word, so Stuck has nothing to keep: the field returns to what the user had written before pressing the button.
  • Undo. After a finished or stopped draft, Undo puts back whatever was in the field when the button was pressed — the user’s own text or the previous draft — and returns to Idle.
  • Busy field. While writing, the field is read-only with aria-busy="true". Next and Back stay enabled, as in Figma; leaving the step stops the writing.
  • Announcements. The message line is a polite live region. Focus goes to Stop writing when writing starts and into the field when it is done or stuck.
  • Counter. 150 characters is the minimum. Below it the counter reads “N of 150 characters”; once it is met, just “N characters”. It counts live, including while the text streams in.
  • Timings. First word after ~0.7 s, then 30–80 ms per word. Slow after 3.5 s without a word, stuck after 8 s. Real values should come from the model’s latency.
  • Reduced motion. The tint and the spinner stand still; the text still streams.
